BAR Rewind has two programs for two different jobs, and you only need the one for what you want to do. Both run alongside your own BAR install, on Windows or Linux.

Player

For watching replays

Opens a parsed replay in your own BAR with a timeline: jump anywhere, play backwards, change speed.

Get the player

Worker

For lending your machine

Parses queued replays for this site in the background while your machine is idle, so others can watch them.

Get the worker
